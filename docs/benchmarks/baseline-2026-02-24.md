## Notes

- The Feb 22 duration regression (+6.8%) was noise from n=2. With n=7, duration shows a clear -20.8% improvement for with-context.
- `test-date-utils` is the highest-variance task: with-context ranged $0.41-$0.93, without-context ranged $0.66-$2.19. This task benefits most from clarte context because without it the agent does significantly more exploration.
- The `without-context` arm has no CLAUDE.md at all. The `with-context` arm uses the full clarte-generated CLAUDE.md.
